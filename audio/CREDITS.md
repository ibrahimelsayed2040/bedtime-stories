# مصادر الأصوات — Audio credits

## الراوي والشخصيات — Narration and character voices
Recorded offline with [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) using open Arabic Piper voices:
- **kareem** (`ar_JO-kareem-medium`) — narrator «الحكواتي», boys' voices, Arnoob. Dataset: https://github.com/AliMokhammad/arabicttstrain
- **dii** (`ar_JO-SA_dii-high`) — narrator «الراوية», girls' and women's voices. OpenVoiceOS, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- **miro** (`ar_JO-SA_miro_V2-high`) — deep voices for big characters. OpenVoiceOS, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

The voice models are licensed for non-commercial use only, so this site must stay free and non-commercial.
